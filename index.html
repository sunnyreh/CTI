01 Why trust, why now

Trust has become both the price of entry and the licence to grow

Health players increasingly engage consumers directly, while expectations are set by consumer-native industries. Yet leadership teams still lack a consistent way to quantify trust, diagnose its drivers and track progress.

Consumerisation changes the benchmark

The shift to direct-to-consumer engagement means health brands are judged against the simplicity and responsiveness of leading consumer experiences

Trust is increasingly public and fragile

Digital channels and event-driven scrutiny amplify changes in perception quickly and visibly

There is no shared trust KPI

Leaders need a credible, repeatable benchmark to understand the gap, identify the levers and measure movement over time

PwC response

A flagship benchmark that creates a repeatable market moment

CTI makes trust visible and actionable, positioning PwC to own the health industries trust conversation and translate insight into client engagement.

  • Launch a distinctive point of view and spark stakeholder attention
  • Anchor executive conversations in proprietary consumer evidence
  • Connect trust gaps to clear intervention pathways and downstream work
  • Build credibility and value as the dataset compounds year on year
